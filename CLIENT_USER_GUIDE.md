## Shopping for Products

### Browsing the Shop

1. Click **"Shop"** in the navigation menu
2. Browse our selection of bikes, parts, and accessories

### Using Filters

On the Shop page, you can filter products by:
- **Search:** Type to search by name or description
- **Category:** Select a category (Mountain Bikes, Road Bikes, Parts, etc.)

## Participating in Auctions

### How Auctions Work

1. Browse active auctions on the **"Auctions"** page
2. Click on an item to view details and current bid
3. Place your bid (must be higher than current bid)
4. Watch the auction in real-time

### Bidding Rules

- You must be logged in to bid
- Each bid must be higher than the current highest bid
- **You cannot bid if you're already the highest bidder**
- **If someone bids in the last 5 minutes, the auction extends by 60 seconds**
- The highest bidder when time runs out wins!

### Tips for Winning

1. **Set a budget** before you start bidding
2. **Watch the timer** - auctions can extend!
3. **Don't wait until the last second** - sniping protection will extend the auction
4. **Check your email** - you'll be notified if you're outbid

### After Winning

1. You'll receive an email confirmation
2. Go to your profile to complete payment
3. Upload proof of payment
4. Arrange pickup or delivery

### Need Help?

Visit our **"How to Bid"** page (link in footer) for a step-by-step guide.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Orders

**Q: How do I track my order?**
A: Go to "My Profile" → "My Orders" to see real-time status updates.

**Q: Can I cancel my order?**
A: Contact us immediately if you need to cancel. Orders that have shipped cannot be cancelled.

**Q: How long does delivery take?**
A: Typically 2-5 business days depending on your location.

### Auctions

**Q: What happens if I win?**
A: You'll be notified by email. Complete payment within 48 hours to claim your item.

**Q: Why did the auction extend?**
A: If someone bids in the last 5 minutes, we add 60 seconds to give everyone a fair chance.

**Q: Can I retract a bid?**
A: No, all bids are binding. Please bid carefully.

### Rentals

**Q: What if I return the bike late?**
A: Additional days are charged at the daily rate.

**Q: Do you provide locks and helmets?**
A: Yes! These are available for rent at checkout.

**Q: What if the bike is damaged?**
A: Report any issues immediately. You're responsible for damages during your rental period.

### Repairs

**Q: How long does a repair take?**
A: Basic tune-up: same day. Performance Pro: 1-2 days. Overhaul Elite: 3-5 days.

**Q: Do you offer warranties on repairs?**
A: Yes, all repairs come with a 30-day warranty on workmanship.

**Q: Can I bring my own parts?**
A: Absolutely! We're happy to install parts you provide.
